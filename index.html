Asendio · AI Engineer & team lead

AI coaching agent & migration to a multi-tenant agent platform

Problem
A live AI coaching agent, used by learners, team leaders and admins, had to move onto a new shared, multi-tenant agent platform — without users noticing anything.
What I built
  • Lead a team of 4 engineers on the migration and own the plan and the switch-over decisions.

A migration designed for zero disruption: the new platform is checked against the live agent before anyone switches, and users get more relevant, personalised help.

Asendio · AI Engineer

Knowledge base & RAG pipeline

Problem
An AI coach is only as good as the knowledge behind it. Customers needed to upload their own documents — PDFs, Office files, even audio — safely, and have the AI find the right parts when answering.
What I built
  • Designed and built the pipeline end to end: secure ingestion with malware scanning, text extraction and OCR for PDF, Office and audio, chunking, embeddings and semantic search.
  • Delivered as scalable, queue-driven Python microservices with tracing, so every document can be followed through the pipeline.
  • Parity testing against the old system before the switch-over.
Outcome
Company knowledge becomes safe, searchable context for the AI — and the switch-over was backed by evidence that the new pipeline matched the old one.

MaskFlare · Founding Engineer

AI data protection for LLM traffic

Problem
Companies with strict EU and Swiss privacy needs (GDPR, healthcare, finance) want to use AI, but can't send personal data, secrets or customer records to LLM providers.
What I built
  • One of the core engineers building the product from zero: sensitive data is masked before it reaches OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Mistral and others, then restored in the answer — without storing prompts.
  • Hybrid PII detection: deterministic rules plus a local machine-learning NER model, with Kosovo/Albania-specific detection and fewer false positives.
  • Masked streaming responses and live voice, with fail-safe behaviour: no data leaks if a model is down.
  • A secure file-scanning service, built almost alone: encrypted quarantine, safe archive unpacking, malware detection and clear, explainable verdicts.
  • Browser data-loss prevention: scans documents uploaded to AI tools, inspects ChatGPT and Claude answers, and discovers AI apps in use with a risk score.
Outcome
Teams can use the AI tools they want while sensitive data stays protected — in chat, streaming, voice and file uploads — with secure, fail-closed defaults.
